spacimore Bottom navigation
A typical structure could have five main navigation tabs:
Tab	Purpose
Home	Discover products, deals, banners and recommendations
Categories	Browse product categories
Wishlist	Saved products
Cart	Items ready to purchase
Account	Orders, addresses, profile, settings



A. Shopper Home 

The home screen is the marketplace discovery engine.

Screen sections:
- Search bar — search products, brands, shops.
- Location/delivery address selector.
- Promotional banners.
- Flash sales / limited deals.
- Shop by category.
- Recommended products.
- Best sellers.
- Recently viewed.
- Sponsored products.
- Official stores / top vendors.
- Popular brands.
- Recently added products.


B. Categories

The category screen organizes the marketplace.
Main category	Subcategories
Electronics	Phones, laptops, televisions, accessories
Fashion	Men, women, shoes, clothing
Home & Living	Furniture, kitchen, décor
Appliances	Refrigerators, washing machines, generators
Beauty	Skincare, makeup, hair
Grocery	Food, beverages, household essentials
Sports	Fitness, equipment, outdoor
Baby & Kids	Toys, clothes, baby care
Automotive	Parts, accessories, tools


C. Search & product discovery

Search functionality:
- Search by product name.
- Search by brand.
- Search by vendor/store.
- Search suggestions.
- Recent searches.
- Trending searches.
- Filters: price, brand, rating, location, delivery speed.
- Sort: popularity, price ascending, price descending, newest, ratings.



D. Product details page

When a shopper selects a product:
- Product image gallery.
- Product name.
- Price.
- Discount and percentage savings.
- Product specifications.
- Available variants (colour, size, model).
- Stock status.
- Seller/store information.
- Seller ratings.
- Product ratings and reviews.
- Delivery estimate.
- Shipping fees.
- Return policy.
- Quantity selector.
- Add to Cart.
- Buy Now.
- Wishlist.
- Share product.
- Frequently bought together.
- Similar products.


E. Cart

The cart should support:
- Multiple vendors.
- Multiple products.
- Quantity adjustments.
- Remove items.
- Save for later.
- Coupon codes.
- Shipping estimates.
- Cart subtotal.
- Discounts.
- Delivery charges.
- Total payable.


Important architecture: Products from different vendors should 
be grouped by vendor internally, even if the shopper sees one combined cart.

F. Checkout


Checkout should be a step-by-step process:
1. Confirm cart items.
2. Choose delivery address.
3. Select delivery option.
4. Choose payment method.
5. Apply coupon or voucher.
6. Confirm order.
7. Complete payment.
8. Receive order confirmation.
Payment options could include:
- Debit card.
- Bank transfer.
- USSD.
- Wallet.
- Buy now, pay later, where supported.
- Cash on delivery, if permitted.



G. Orders

The Orders section should contain:
- Pending payment.
- Confirmed.
- Processing.
- Shipped.
- Ready for pickup.
- Delivered.
- Cancelled.
- Returned.
- Refunded.
Each order should have an order detail page with:
- Order number.
- Items purchased.
- Vendor.
- Payment status.
- Delivery tracking.
- Delivery address.
- Timeline.
- Support.
- Cancellation/return options, where eligible



H. Shopper account
The account screen should contain:
Section	Features
My Orders	Order history and tracking
My Profile	Name, phone, email
Addresses	Home, office, additional addresses
Payment Methods	Saved or supported payment methods
Wishlist	Saved products
Reviews	Product reviews
Coupons	Available discount vouchers
Notifications	Order alerts and promotions
Customer Support	Help, complaints and dispute resolution
Settings	Security, password, privacy

2. Vendor / Seller structure
Jumia separates the selling experience from the shopping experience 
through its Vendor Center. Jumia's Nigerian vendor onboarding also 
describes seller 
registration, verification, training, product listing, promotion 
and analytics

If  were building the vendor app, I would use a dedicated vendor 
navigation structure.
Vendor bottom navigation
For a mobile Vendor Center:
Tab	Purpose
Overview	Sales, orders, earnings and performance
Products	Product catalogue and inventory
Orders	Process customer orders
Finance	Earnings, fees and payouts
Store	Shop profile and settings



A. Vendor dashboard / Overview

This is the vendor's business command centre.
Dashboard cards
- Total sales.
- Today's sales.
- Total orders.
- Pending orders.
- Completed orders.
- Cancelled orders.
- Available balance.
- Pending balance.
- Products in stock.
- Low-stock products.


Dashboard content
- Sales chart.
- Orders chart.
- Sales performance.
- Top-selling products.
- Recent orders.
- Customer reviews.
- Stock alerts.
- Marketplace announcements.
- Promotion opportunities.




B. Products
Product management
Vendors should be able to:
- Add a new product.
- Edit products.
- Delete or archive products.
- Upload multiple images.
- Add videos, where supported.
- Create product variants.
- Set prices.
- Set discount prices.
- Set stock quantities.
- Manage SKU.
- Set product descriptions.
- Add specifications.
- Assign categories.
- Set product visibility.
- Check product approval status.


Product status
Status	Meaning
Draft	Not yet submitted
Pending review	Awaiting marketplace approval
Active	Visible to shoppers
Rejected	Failed marketplace review
Out of stock	No available inventory
Inactive	Hidden from marketplace



C. Vendor orders


The vendor should have an order management centre.
Order tabs
- All orders.
- New orders.
- Processing.
- Ready to ship.
- Shipped.
- Delivered.
- Cancelled.
- Returned.


Vendor order actions
- View order details.
- Accept/process order.
- Print invoice.
- Prepare package.
- Update fulfilment status.
- Mark as ready for pickup.
- Handover to logistics.
- View buyer delivery information as authorized.
- Request cancellation where appropriate.
- Respond to return requests.



The vendor shouldn't be allowed to mark an order 
as paid or delivered arbitrarily. Payment 
confirmation and delivery milestones 
should be controlled by payment and 
logistics integrations, or by verifiable authorized processes.

D. Inventory management


Inventory management should be independent from the product catalogue.
Feature	Description
Stock quantity	Current inventory
Stock adjustment	Add or remove stock
Stock history	Track changes
Low-stock alerts	Alert seller when quantity is low
SKU	Unique stock identifier
Variants	Separate inventory per size/colour
Bulk updates	Modify multiple products
Inventory movement	Track sales, returns and restocking



E. Finance / Wallet

Vendor finance screen
- Gross sales.
- Marketplace commission.
- Shipping charges.
- Discounts.
- Refunds.
- Net earnings.
- Pending settlement.
- Available payout.
- Payout history.
- Bank account.
- Financial statements.


Financial flow
Shopper pays for order


Marketplace payment processing
Payment confirmation, reconciliation and order association

Vendor settlement calculation
Commission, applicable fees, refunds and adjustments

Vendor payout to bank account


The vendor should see exactly how the amount payable was calculated.
